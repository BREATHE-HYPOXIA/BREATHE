B.R.E.A.T.H.E.
Biological Risk Evaluation and Aquatic Trends for Hypoxia Events

B.R.E.A.T.H.E. is a water-quality monitoring system designed to continuously observe important aquatic parameters and help identify environmental conditions associated with hypoxia and potential fish-kill events.

Early Warning

Continuously monitor dissolved oxygen and other water-quality parameters to help identify potentially dangerous conditions before they become severe.

Water Quality Monitoring

Track dissolved oxygen, temperature, pH, and turbidity through a centralized monitoring dashboard.

Environmental Analysis

Examine relationships between water-quality parameters through trends and correlation analysis.

Data Privacy

Data collected by the system is intended primarily for academic research, device testing, and environmental monitoring purposes.
